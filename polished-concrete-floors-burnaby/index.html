Questions to ask about polished concrete floors Burnaby

Start by looking at the concrete you already have. Is it covered, stained, cracked, uneven or simply looking tired? The slab's condition affects what can be achieved, so ask for an assessment before settling on a finish. Polishing refines the existing surface, but it doesn't make every slab look identical or erase every mark.

Talk through how the room is used. A busy entry, a living area and a basement may call for different expectations around appearance and upkeep. Ask what the proposed finish will look like in your space, how the work will affect access to the room, and what care the finished floor needs. These are useful questions for anyone comparing polished concrete floors Burnaby homes may have.

A short checklist before you hire

Keep these points handy when you speak with a concrete polishing contractor:

  • Ask them to inspect the slab and explain any visible concerns.
  • Describe the look you want, including how much of the concrete's natural variation you'd like to see.
  • Find out what preparation and polishing steps are included in the work being discussed.
  • Ask how long the room may be out of use and what you should move beforehand.
  • Discuss routine cleaning so you know how to care for the surface afterward.

A clear conversation is more useful than choosing from a finish description alone. If you have photos of the room or know about past repairs, share that information during the discussion.

What to prepare at home

Clear small furniture, rugs and personal items from the work area when you are asked to prepare the room. Make a plan for pets and household members to stay away from the work zone. If the floor is in a basement or another room with limited access, mention that early so the contractor can talk through the arrangement.

Point out stains, cracks, patched areas or places that feel uneven. Don't cover a concern with paint or another coating just before an assessment. Let the contractor see the actual surface and explain anything you know about its history. This helps set realistic expectations for polished concrete floors Burnaby residents are considering for an existing slab.

Mistakes to avoid, and when to call a pro

One common mistake is choosing a floor finish based only on a picture. The concrete in your home has its own condition and character, so ask how those may affect the result. Another is assuming polishing will correct a structural problem or make a damaged slab uniform. Get advice before deciding how to handle cracks, movement or significant unevenness.

Call a professional if you're unsure what is on the concrete, if a coating is present, or if the floor has damage you can't assess. An on-site conversation can help clarify whether polishing fits your goals and what preparation may be needed. Questions people ask about polished concrete floors Burnaby

Can every concrete slab be polished?

Not every slab will produce the same finish. The condition of the concrete and any existing coating or repairs should be assessed before you decide.

What should I move before polishing work?

Plan to clear furniture and belongings from the work area as arranged with the contractor. Ask about access and keeping people and pets away while the floor is being worked on.

Is polished concrete hard to look after?

It is generally chosen as a low-maintenance flooring option, but it still needs routine care. Ask what cleaning approach suits the finish in your home.
