Start with the driveway’s condition

Before contacting a concrete contractor driveway Burnaby homeowners should look over the entire surface, including the edges and the area where the driveway meets the garage or street. Note cracks, loose or flaking areas, low spots, and places where water seems to sit. A close-up photo can help explain what you’re seeing, but it can’t replace an on-site assessment.

Think about how the driveway is used, too. Consider the number and size of vehicles, whether anyone needs room to turn, and how people walk from the car to the entrance. These details help you explain the problem and the result you’re hoping for. If the existing concrete is badly uneven or breaking apart in several places, ask whether a local repair is sensible or if the wider surface needs attention.

Questions to ask before hiring

When you speak with a concrete contractor driveway Burnaby residents can ask what work is included and what conditions might change the recommended approach. Request a clear explanation of the steps, such as how the area will be prepared, how access to the home may be affected, and what care the finished concrete will need.

A useful checklist for your first conversation:

  • Which parts of the driveway are included in the assessment?
  • How will drainage and the driveway’s connection to nearby surfaces be considered?
  • What should be moved or kept clear before work starts?
  • Is polishing suitable for this concrete, or does the surface need another kind of work?

Polishing is intended for concrete surfaces that are appropriate for that finish. Ask directly whether it fits your driveway’s condition and use instead of assuming every outdoor slab can be polished.

Get the property ready

Before an assessment, clear parked vehicles and movable items from the driveway if you can. Keep pets and children away from the area while the contractor is looking at the surface. Make a short note of any concerns you’ve noticed, including where water collects or where the surface feels uneven underfoot.

Also mention practical limits at the property, such as narrow access or a shared entrance. If neighbours use part of the space, explain that as well. The more accurately you describe how the driveway functions, the easier it is to discuss a suitable scope and plan around access.

Avoid rushed decisions

Don’t judge a proposal by a brief look at one visible crack. Ask how the overall surface, edges, drainage, and intended use factor into the recommendation. Be cautious about agreeing to a finish before confirming that it suits the concrete and the conditions it faces outdoors.

Call a professional when cracking spreads, sections shift, water regularly pools, or the surface is becoming difficult to use safely. Questions people ask about concrete contractor driveway Burnaby

Can every concrete driveway be polished?

No, the right finish depends on the concrete’s condition and the setting. Ask for an assessment before deciding that polishing is suitable for an outdoor driveway.

What should I tell a concrete contractor about my driveway?

Describe how many vehicles use it, where people walk, and any trouble spots such as standing water or uneven sections. Photos and notes can help you explain what you’ve observed.

When should I call a professional about driveway concrete?

Reach out if damage is spreading, sections have shifted, water is collecting, or walking and driving over the surface has become difficult. An assessment can help clarify what kind of work may be appropriate.
