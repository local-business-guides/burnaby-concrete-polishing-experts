Questions for a contractor for concrete Burnaby homeowners

Before changing an older concrete floor, decide what you need the space to do and what you’d like the surface to look like. That gives you a more useful conversation with a local contractor.

Begin with the room, not a finish

When looking for a contractor for concrete Burnaby homeowners can start by explaining how a room is used. A floor in a garage, basement, or main living area may face different routines and demands. Think about who uses the space, what gets placed on the floor, and which parts are hardest to maintain.

Then describe what is bothering you about the concrete. Perhaps the surface looks dull, has visible wear, or no longer suits the room. Polishing may be worth discussing if you want to work with the existing slab rather than cover it. The floor needs to be reviewed before anyone can speak confidently about what may be possible.

Compare the scope of work

A useful conversation should cover more than the final look. Ask what the contractor plans to assess, what preparation may be involved, and how the room could be affected while the floor is being worked on. If you have questions about access or moving belongings, bring them up before scheduling.

Use this checklist when discussing the project:

  • Explain the room’s everyday use and any busy areas.
  • Point out cracks, old repairs, stains, or sections that feel uneven.
  • Ask whether those conditions may affect polishing.
  • Confirm what you should move or protect before work starts.
  • Ask what routine care is appropriate for the finished floor.

You can also share a few clear photos if you’re first asking about the service. Photos may help start the discussion, but they don’t replace an assessment of the actual concrete. Be wary of making a decision based on a finish example alone, since your slab may have different conditions.

Plan around your household

Think ahead about how the room will be used during the work. Move breakable items and clear a route to the area. If furniture, storage, or appliances are difficult to shift, tell the contractor before the project is planned. Discuss how nearby rooms are reached and whether anyone needs regular access through the space.

Keep your goals simple and specific. You might want a cleaner-looking floor, a surface that’s easier to care for, or a finish that fits the rest of the room. Let the contractor know which goal matters most. That makes it easier to talk through practical choices without guessing at what you have in mind.

Know when to ask for professional advice

Contact a concrete professional if you’re uncertain about the slab’s condition, if damage is becoming more noticeable, or if you’re weighing polishing against another flooring option. Don’t try to hide a problem before an assessment. Sharing what you’ve noticed helps the contractor consider the right questions and explain the available approach.

What should I discuss with a contractor for concrete in Burnaby?

Should I send photos of my concrete floor?

Photos can help introduce the floor and your concerns. They may not show every surface condition, so an in-person assessment may still be needed before discussing the work in detail.
